# Media Channels Used in DoorDash Brand Marketing

## Overview

This document identifies all media channels used in DoorDash's brand marketing campaigns based on the Kantar BLS data analysis.

---

## Channel Extraction Methodology

The channels are extracted from the `GROUP_NAME` and `FILTER_NAME` fields in the Kantar data. The analysis system automatically categorizes channels using keyword matching, but this document provides a detailed breakdown of all channels found in the raw data.

---

## Channel Strategy Insights

### Multi-Channel Approach

DoorDash is using a **comprehensive multi-channel strategy** with presence across:

- ✅ **Digital/Programmatic** (The Trade Desk - 7,125+ obs, Realm, Trip Advisor)
- ✅ **Video** (YouTube, OTT/CTV)
- ✅ **Social Media** (Facebook/Meta DI - 994 obs)
- ✅ **Audio/Podcast** (iHeart Podcast - 868 obs, Good Karma Podcast - 140 obs)
- ✅ **Streaming/OTT** (Amazon CTV across multiple markets)
- ✅ **Traditional TV** (Multiple TV networks tracked)
- ✅ **Publishers** (Hearst Corporation and others)

### Channel Mix

The data shows:
- **Primary focus**: Digital and programmatic channels (The Trade Desk is the largest with 7,125+ observations)
- **Strong presence**: Podcast/audio channels (iHeart: 868 obs, Good Karma: 140 obs)
- **Active**: Social media (Facebook: 994 obs)
- **Growing**: Video platforms (YouTube, OTT/CTV - Amazon CTV across 5+ markets)
- **Supporting**: Traditional TV networks (6,894+ obs per network)
- **International**: Amazon CTV presence in US, DE, FR, BR, MX markets

### Creative Variations

Multiple creative formats are being tested:
- Static ads
- Video ads
- Animation
- Different placements (Stories, Feed, etc.)
- Publisher-specific variations
